Previous analysis on the topic of di-jets in UPC has been done \cite{collaboration_measurement_2025}. The ATLAS analysis, while looking for $\gamma A$ di-jet behavior with nuclear breakup, noticed that the cross section for di-jet events without nuclear breakup is much higher than anticipated. This increase in cross section is attributed to diffractive events, in which the photon interacts not with the nucleus itself but rather with a pomeron emitted by the probed nucleus.
