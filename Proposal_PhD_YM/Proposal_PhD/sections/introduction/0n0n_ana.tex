In this analysis we will examine data from 2018 Pb + Pb run. The aim of this analysis is to resolve ambiguities regarding the direction of the emitted photons in 0n0n events, as well as the ability to differentiate between different processes through the event topology. In addition, I propose to explore both basic and uncommon observables through the lens of the event topology, and aim to extract additional information on the event. The work already completed, which will be addressed in the upcoming chapter, concerns the use of MC to establish methods for photon direction discrimination, as well as an attempt to differentiate between $\gamma A$, $\gamma \mathbb{P}$, and $\gamma\gamma$ samples. The planned work ahead, which will be described in the last chapter, will lay out the proposed research to improve upon these methods, as well as use them to extract new information from available data.
